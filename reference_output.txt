The quick brown fox was walking through the forest. He was looking for something to eat. Suddenly, he saw a big, juicy apple. He was so excited that he started to jump up and down.
The fox was so happy that he started to jump up and down. He wanted to eat the apple right away. But then he heard a voice.
"Hey, fox! What are you doing?"
The fox looked up and saw a little girl. She was wearing a bright yellow dress.
"I'm looking for something to eat," said the fox.
The little girl smiled. "I know where you can find some delicious apples. Follow me!"
The fox followed the little girl to a tree. She pointed to a big, juicy apple.
The fox was so excited that he jumped up and down. He grabbed the apple and started to eat it.
The little girl laughed. "You're so lucky! You found the perfect apple!"
The fox smiled and thanked the little girl. He was so happy that he had found something to eat.
